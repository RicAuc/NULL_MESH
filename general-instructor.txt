
Scientific infographic combined with restrained avant-garde graphic design.

Two orthogonal semi-transparent hyperplanes rendered as geometric fields of fine-grained noise or sparse dot matrices, expressed in contrasting registers. One plane appears in cold white or light grey with precise structural linework, representing the real substrate. The other appears in deep black with faint iridescent cyan traces that dissolve towards the edges, representing the spectral register.

The two planes converge at a single vanishing point labelled with the empty set symbol ∅. This node is minimal and softly luminous, neither warm nor cold. The convergence remains geometrically quiet and unemphasised.

Around the convergence point, introduce a subtle local warping of the grid on both planes. The deformation should suggest a slight change in the metric, comparable to a restrained gravitational lensing effect, only visible on close inspection.

Place minimal typographic labels near each plane in a clean monospace or grotesque typeface:
“real register” on the structural plane,
“spectral register” on the dark plane.
Use small capitals with low contrast so that typography remains secondary to geometry.
