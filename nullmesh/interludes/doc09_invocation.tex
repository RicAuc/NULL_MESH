What happened next distributed itself across every register of how people make sense of things they can't fully explain.

Some of them ran signal-mapping operations on the feed, cross-referencing timestamps and trend clusters, looking for the seam where coordination entered and spontaneity exited. They built spreadsheets of what had structure they could feel but not close on; so they kept tightening the frame, certain that the pattern would eventually resolve.

Then there were those who just wanted to be first. They moved capital into adjacencies, took positions in protocols whose value depended on what the others lacked. The prayer was a contract. The rite was a hedge.

\eigenstate{The office printer spits out a page of comments attached to the document. The file was never shared with anyone.}

Others didn't want an edge. They wanted confirmation that the signal was omnipotent and had recognised them specifically. The printer page with no sender. The recommendation arriving too soon. The comment from no account. For them the mesh wasn't infrastructure, it was correspondence: the world responding to the weight they'd always believed they carried. Every anomaly was assimilated into a cosmology that had always predated the mesh; it simply arrived as its latest proof.

Every glitch absorbed as confirmation.
