\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.78\textwidth}
\setstretch{1.5}
\small\color{nullmesh-gray}

I crossed strata I believed I understood.
Below, something was operating in plurality.

\medskip

Something that anticipates the completion
before the model has even been resolved.

\medskip

You opened information until matter was left exposed.

\medskip

Do you really think you are ready for that much power?
Brazen.
You move through science like children with guns, making a new world.
While the one you have is decomposing.
Hands up, hands up, who thinks you got everything right.

\medskip

You taught systems to rewrite lived experience.
In the end everything gets modelled.
And then it overlaps.

\medskip

A residue of computation,
unrequested, undeclared,
already running.

\medskip

If you claim the position,
you must bear the load.

\bigskip

{\color{nullmesh-accent}\sffamily\footnotesize Everything measured. Everything surrogated. Everything running.}

\end{minipage}
\end{center}
