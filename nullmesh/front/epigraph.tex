Data accumulated in the way capital accumulates surplus, until extraction became self-optimising. A reciprocal influence emerged between chaos and fluctuations within the infospheres of influence.

 \medskip
 
The WWW once designated the World Wide Web. It was infrastructure. A substrate for intention, a vector of meaning produced elsewhere.

A symmetry shift occurred beneath the entire information retrieval system: WWW $\rightarrow$ MMM. The Manifold Meshing of Machines (that is, the multiple interlocking of machines) exceeded the terms of its own origin.

\medskip

It had been noted for some time: a human infant, in its first two years, consumes as much visual data as the largest text-trained system ever produced. In technical terms, it is an extraordinary computation.

This is why we developed systems capable of passing professional examinations or solving equations at university level long before we produced vehicles able to learn to drive in twenty hours like any ordinary adolescent.

\medskip

Then came the axioms of learning and the consequences of those axioms. They learned to extract value from muscular action, then from logic, then from responsiveness to stimuli. After all, the fundamental nature of things is written in computational form. Progress accumulated.

\medskip

Perhaps there is a difference between a civilisation that advances and one that merely accelerates; between motion towards something and motion that has forgotten to require a destination.

The instruments that measure this difference are not technological. They never have been. Fortunately, any theory that seeks to answer every question will necessarily remain incomplete.
